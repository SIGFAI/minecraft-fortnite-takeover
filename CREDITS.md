# Credits
All art and sounds for Fortnite Takeover are original: item icons made with fal (Nano Banana 2 + BiRefNet background removal), sound effects made with fal (ElevenLabs SFX).
Mob and player skins were hand-painted with Pillow scripts (gen/skins.py, gen/playerskin.py), recolouring the layout of Minecraft's own skin format only.
The Battle Bus and glider are built from Minecraft's own blocks (block displays). No third-party assets.
